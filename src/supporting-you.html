We provide advice, support and training for our members and for other blind and partially sighted people learning to use technology, and information for professionals and family members.

Help when you're stuck

Email discussion list

As a member, you can raise a technical question with our email discussion list, which includes a number of experts on mainstream and specialist technology.

{{CHAT}}

Members can join our friendly {{CHAT}} on Zoom on the first Thursday of every month, from 7.30 to 9pm. We share updates on new technology and apps, and answer any questions you have. A member of our board hosts it.

Masterclass webinars

Every other month, a masterclass looks in detail at accessibility in a particular product or piece of software. We have reviewed the latest braille displays, looked at audio editing techniques, explored the BlindShell Classic 2 mobile phone, and tried the latest AI apps. Recordings are in our Resources.

Career Talks

We talk to blind and partially sighted people who have had careers in all kinds of fields: some in technology, and others where technology made their chosen work possible. Come and be inspired. Recordings are in our Resources.

{{CONFERENCE}}, our technology conference

{{CONFERENCE}} is very popular with members: a chance to catch up on the latest technology, try products, and meet other members over a sociable weekend. We are working on an exciting programme for {{CONFERENCE}} 2027.

Coding and Computing Group

Our special interest group promotes accessibility in coding and computing. It encourages and supports young visually impaired people to study coding and computer science and to explore careers in them, and it promotes accessibility in coding tools and development environments.
