{{SERIES}}: five volumes of comic poems and performances, on CD and as audio downloads.

The series was the idea of one of our members, made possible by a folk poet who donated all the material and by many well-known voices who performed it. The first volume was launched in July 2003, and a new one every two years until 2011. Money from every copy helps us support blind and partially sighted people to use technology.

The volumes

  1. {{SERIES}}
  2. Missing Syrians
  3. Top Hamster
  4. Hamster Nav
  5. Herding Hamsters

Each is available on CD or as an audio download, and all five come as a set.

In the finished site, each volume has a page with its tracks, a sample to listen to, and a way to buy it. In this preview, nothing is for sale.
