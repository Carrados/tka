    SightCity Frankfurt claims to be the world’s largest trade fair for aids and solutions for the blind and visually impaired.

    Sight Tech Global is a world-wide, virtual conference dedicated to highlighting technology's power to transform lives for people living with vision loss around the world by bringing together the pioneers who are applying the latest technology advances to barrier-free navigation, human interaction, and low-friction access to information for people who are blind or visually impaired.
