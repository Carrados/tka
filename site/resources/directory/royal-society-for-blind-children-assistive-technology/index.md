For Children and Young People up to the age of 25, this charity can arrange a one to one session for you and your family with their Assistive Technology Officer.

Sessions can take place at their Life Without Limits Centre in London or delivered online. All sessions last between 60 and 90 minutes and can be arranged at a convenient date and time.
