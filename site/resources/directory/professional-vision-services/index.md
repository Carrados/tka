Professional Vision Services was incorporated in 1990 by Philippa Wisbey - Managing Director and owner, importing the Magnilink brand of video magnifiers and reading machines from Low Vision International (LVI) into the UK. We are one of the oldest companies within the Visual Impairment field today.

Professional Vision Services own a substantial amount of LVI and therefore are in the unique position in the UK of designing and manufacturing products for the visually impaired market. To name one; The Magnilink Student Addition - which won the prestigious education BETT award and is still the only visually impaired product to win this award. Other products such as the Magnilink ZIP evolved from customer consultations and their requests.

All products can be demonstrated free of charge on site throughout the UK by qualified experienced staff. We can offer full installation and training on all products.

Professional Vision Services has a vast experience of the Employment and Education marketplace and can create bespoke solutions for every situation.
