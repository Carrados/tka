Help a child or young person

Preview
This is a private preview of a website being built for a real charity, under an invented name. Names, pictures and numbers on it are made up.

7 entries, A to Z.

  • Guide Dogs: Tech for All
    At Guide Dogs we believe all children with a vision impairment should have access to technology.
  • RNIB: Digital Skills Team
    RNIB's Digital Skills team can help with all kinds of requests including information, advice and guidance over the phone, over email or with a volunteer.
  • RNIB: Technology for Independent Learning
    The RNIB provide an overview of the role of technology in the development of a child or young person, the role of the QTVI (Qualified Teachers of children and young people with Vision Impairment), and provide information on some the mainstream and specialist solutions which are available.
  • Royal Society for Blind Children: Assistive Technology
    For Children and Young People up to the age of 25, this charity can arrange a one to one session for you and your family with their Assistive Technology Officer.
  • Thomas Pocklington Trust: Education Technology
    Whether you’re at school, college or university, technology plays a vital role in your education.
  • VICTA grant scheme for children and young adults
    For visually impaired children and young people aged 8 to 29, VICTA can provide grants for laptops, software and items for independent living for home use.
  • Visably
    This charity (previously known as British Wireless for the Blind Fund) provides audio devices, radios and audiobook players to visually impaired adults and children for free.
