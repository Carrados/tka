Facebook Group: NVDA screen reader users

Preview
This is a private preview of a website being built for a real charity, under an invented name. Names, pictures and numbers on it are made up.

This group provides NVDA users different things, add-ons, NVDA versions, discussions, and sharing opinions. NVDA was created by people, and they called NVAccess. NVDA is a screen reader for blind and visually impaired people, it helps them to brows their computers smoothly, reading bookx using this screen reader with synthesizers - text to speach survice (TTS).

As well as this NVDA supports many languages, including Arabic, Spanish, English, Chinese and another languages. I would advise you to visit their website to know about them if you are a new NVDA user.

http://www.nvaccess.org/
