At AAT Solutions, we specialize in helping visually impaired individuals use assistive technology confidently and effectively. As a certified trainer who is blind, I bring a unique perspective and personal experience that greatly enhance our training.

Personalized Training: We offer training sessions tailored to your needs. Our focus includes key tools like JAWS, NVDA, ZoomText, and Dragon NaturallySpeaking. We make sure you can use these tools easily in your everyday life, whether at home or work.

Unique Advantage: My personal experience as a blind individual helps me understand the real challenges of using assistive technology. This allows me to offer practical and empathetic support, helping you overcome obstacles and build confidence with tailored strategies.

Broad Device Training: Besides assistive technologies, we also provide training on popular platforms like Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. This helps you become comfortable with various types of technology, improving your overall digital skills and readiness.

Hands-On Learning: Our sessions focus on practical, hands-on learning. You’ll practice using your new skills in real-life situations, making it easier to apply what you’ve learned effectively.

Ongoing Support: We are dedicated to your success. We offer continuous support and encouragement, helping you build confidence and address any challenges you may face as you use your new skills.
