3 entries, A to Z.

- [DATEurope](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/dateurope/) DATEurope is the recently founded European Industry Association for Digital Assistive Technology.
- [UKAAF (UK Association for Accessible Formats)](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/ukaaf-uk-association-for-accessible-formats/) TKA is a Sponsoring Member of UKAAF, the industry association that sets standards and promotes best practice for accessible documents in the UK.
