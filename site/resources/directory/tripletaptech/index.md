TripleTapTech is a dedicated resource for individuals with visual impairments, providing expert advice, support, and training on all things technology. We believe that technology should be accessible to everyone, and we’re committed to helping you harness its power to enhance your independence and quality of life. They can provide one to to one training online or over the phone, through their helpline or via pre-arranged home visits.
