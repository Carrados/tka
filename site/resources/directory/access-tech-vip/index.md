Access Tech VIP is led by Adrian Tamasan, a blind assistive technology trainer with more than 25 years of experience using screen readers across Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and specialist audio workflows.

Training can be provided on screen reader basics and advanced navigation techniques, email, web browsing, managing files, and accessibility on tablets and smartphones.

Adrian can provide face to face training in England and internationally for one to one online training, in English or Romanian.
