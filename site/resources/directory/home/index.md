4 entries, A to Z.

- [AbilityNet](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/abilitynet-information-and-advice/) AbilityNet is a UK-based charity with a global reach - our vision is a digital world accessible to all.
- [Computer Room Services](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/computer-room-services/) We offer training on all the main screen reading packages and also on a wide variety of software including WordPress.
- [RNIB: Digital Skills Team](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/rnib-technology-for-life-team/) RNIB's Digital Skills team can help with all kinds of requests including information, advice and guidance over the phone, over email or with a volunteer.
- [TripleTapTech](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/tripletaptech/) TripleTapTech is a dedicated resource for individuals with visual impairments, providing expert advice, support, and training on all things technology.
