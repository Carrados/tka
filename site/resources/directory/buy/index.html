  • Computer Room Services
    We offer training on all the main screen reading packages and also on a wide variety of software including WordPress.
  • Dolphin Computer Access
    Dolphin assistive technology products are designed to improve accessibility and independence on computers, smartphones and tablets for people with a wide range of vision impairments.
  • Humanware
    For over 30 years, HumanWare’s inspirational vision has resulted in a range of highly intuitive and intelligent solutions that empower people who are blind or with low vision by giving them the independence to participate effectively within a sighted world.
  • Optelec
    We are one of the UK’s largest and leaders in manufacturing Low Vision Technology for all areas of sight loss.
  • Pamtrad UK
    Pamtrad UK are a provider of a wide range of specialist equipment, from electronic magnifiers and text to speech systems to computer access software.
  • Professional Vision Services
    Professional Vision Services was incorporated in 1990 by Philippa Wisbey - Managing Director and owner, importing the Magnilink brand of video magnifiers and reading machines from Low Vision International (LVI) into the UK.
