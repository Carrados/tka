12 entries, A to Z.

- [Aspire Consultancy](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/aspire-consultancy/) Aspire Consultancy is an independent company that provides quality advice, assessment and a training service on all aspects of assistive technology for visually impaired people.
- [Computer Room Services](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/computer-room-services/) We offer training on all the main screen reading packages and also on a wide variety of software including WordPress.
- [Dolphin Computer Access](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/dolphin-computer-access/) Dolphin assistive technology products are designed to improve accessibility and independence on computers, smartphones and tablets for people with a wide range of vision impairments.
- [Humanware](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/humanware/) For over 30 years, HumanWare’s inspirational vision has resulted in a range of highly intuitive and intelligent solutions that empower people who are blind or with low vision by giving them the independence to participate effectively within a sighted world.
- [Optelec](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/optelec/) We are one of the UK’s largest and leaders in manufacturing Low Vision Technology for all areas of sight loss.
- [Pamtrad UK](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/pamtrad-uk/) Pamtrad UK are a provider of a wide range of specialist equipment, from electronic magnifiers and text to speech systems to computer access software.
- [Professional Vision Services](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/professional-vision-services/) Professional Vision Services was incorporated in 1990 by Philippa Wisbey - Managing Director and owner, importing the Magnilink brand of video magnifiers and reading machines from Low Vision International (LVI) into the UK.
- [Ray-Ban Meta Glasses](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/rayban/) Useful links:
- [RNIB: Digital Skills Team](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/rnib-technology-for-life-team/) RNIB's Digital Skills team can help with all kinds of requests including information, advice and guidance over the phone, over email or with a volunteer.
- [Sight &amp; Sound Technology](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/sight-sound-technology/) Sight and Sound Technology is an accredited training provider with resources across the UK and Ireland.
- [Visably](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/visably/) This charity (previously known as British Wireless for the Blind Fund) provides audio devices, radios and audiobook players to visually impaired adults and children for free.
- [VisionAid Technologies](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/visionaid-technologies/) VisionAid Technologies Ltd is a family owned &amp; run business who have been providing solutions to assist partially sighted &amp; blind people since 1996.
