Davinder Kullar from Personaleyes offers tailored training, support and coaching to blind and partially sighted people on a range of technology, such as computers, smart phones, tablets through to smart speakers and everyday living solutions. This is done in a supportive manner to enable people to be effective with the tools they own and they offer them quality and value to their life.

Examples of accessibility training includes

• Screen readers such as Windows Narrator, NVDA and JAWS on desktop

• Voiceover iOS and talkback on Android for smart phones and tablets

• Low vision adaptations such as Magnification on desktops, smart phones and tablets

• Advice on everyday living devices and options on acquiring them, such as in home kitchen and other tasks.

Accessibility consultancy is offered on aspects from website user testing for access needs, assessing physical environments and low vision training to people that interact with blind and partially sighted individuals, such as professionals through to friends and families. This is to ensure the best interactions occur and people feel comfortable with the knowledge and skills for the situations, environments and tools they need access to.

Coaching is provided with my personal years of living with a health condition and utilising a range of knowledge and experience to provide a space to go through situations, options/ approaches and support the action to implement them. Coaching is aimed to develop the person to where they would like to be in life.
