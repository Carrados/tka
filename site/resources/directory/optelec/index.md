We are one of the UK’s largest and leaders in manufacturing Low Vision Technology for all areas of sight loss.

Our services are personal to your needs, whether it be magnification, reading machines, optics or software.

We have Low vision specialists all throughout the UK and Ireland who can offer free home demonstrations on our products.
