SightCity Frankfurt claims to be the world’s largest trade fair for aids and solutions for the blind and visually impaired. Every year, Kap Europa in Frankfurt becomes an international center for innovation, inclusion and progress. As a hybrid event, SightCity combines digital and on-site formats, providing a first-class platform for experts, those affected and interested parties.
