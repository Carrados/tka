7 entries, A to Z.

- [Blatchington Court Trust: Grant Guidelines](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/blatchington-court-trust-grant-guidelines/) Applications will be considered for children and young people who are registered or are registrable as blind (SSI) or partially sighted (SI) up to and including the age of 29 years and who reside permanently in Sussex.
- [Blind Aid](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/blind-aid/) Living with blindness or visual impairment brings many challenges to everyday life.
- [Gardner's Trust for the Blind](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/gardners-trust-for-the-blind/) The Gardner's Trust for the Blind provides financial assistance to registered blind and partially sighted individuals in the UK.
- [Guide Dogs: Tech for All](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/guide-dogs-tech-4-all/) At Guide Dogs we believe all children with a vision impairment should have access to technology.
- [RNIB: Grants](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/rnib-grants/) RNIB offer grants to registered blind or partially sighted people for useful technology that can help them live independently.
- [The Powell Family Foundation (TPFF)](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/powell-family-foundation/) The aim of The Powell Family Foundation is to support vision impaired children, young people and adults aged 25 and under, to improve their emotional and physical well-being, increase their knowledge and skills, and support their independence through the provision of grants to individuals and charitable organisations.
- [VICTA grant scheme for children and young adults](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/victa-grant-scheme-for-children-and-young-adults/) For visually impaired children and young people aged 8 to 29, VICTA can provide grants for laptops, software and items for independent living for home use.
