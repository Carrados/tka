Dolphin assistive technology products are designed to improve accessibility and independence on computers, smartphones and tablets for people with a wide range of vision impairments. This includes screen readers for people who are blind, magnification and audio products for people who have partial sight and low vision and also apps to access audio and digital books on a wide range of platforms.
