Justin Macleod

Preview
This is a private preview of a website being built for a real charity, under an invented name. Names, pictures and numbers on it are made up.

I have taught students in the areas of audio restoration; mixing; basic mastering, audio fundamentals and concepts; electronic music production; dialogue editing; and sound design. I offer a free consultation where prospective students and I can get a feel for one another and where I can find out what previous experience the student has and what their audio aspirations are so we can go over a possible lesson plan. I use this time to find out the student's preferred learning style as well.

My lessons include, where applicable and if the student wishes, recordings of the lessons; the setting and evaluation of exercises so that they can practice what they've learned between lessons; between lesson support in case they encounter any problems or have questions; and supplementary notes or audio demonstrations of concepts they would like reinforced or to explore further.
