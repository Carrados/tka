At Guide Dogs we believe all children with a vision impairment should have access to technology. Our Tech for All service provides families with the technology they need. This includes Apple iPads, specialist technology and software or our sensory toy bundles to use outside of school (while stocks last). Our expert team can also provide personalised advice about technology to families.
