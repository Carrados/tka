## Advice services

8 entries

- [AbilityNet](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/abilitynet-information-and-advice/) AbilityNet is a UK-based charity with a global reach - our vision is a digital world accessible to all.
- [Connect by Tech Service from the Macular Society](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/connect-by-tech-service-from-the-macular-society/) The Macular Society’s Connect by Tech service offers one-to-one telephone support with the technology people use around their home.
- [Guide Dogs: Tech for All](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/guide-dogs-tech-4-all/) At Guide Dogs we believe all children with a vision impairment should have access to technology.
- [RNIB: Digital Skills Team](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/rnib-technology-for-life-team/) RNIB's Digital Skills team can help with all kinds of requests including information, advice and guidance over the phone, over email or with a volunteer.
- [RNIB: Technology for Independent Learning](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/rnib-technology-for-independent-learning/) The RNIB provide an overview of the role of technology in the development of a child or young person, the role of the QTVI (Qualified Teachers of children and young people with Vision Impairment), and provide information on some the mainstream and specialist solutions which are available.
- [Thomas Pocklington Trust: Education Technology](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/thomas-pocklington-trust-education-technology/) Whether you’re at school, college or university, technology plays a vital role in your education.
- [TripleTapTech](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/tripletaptech/) TripleTapTech is a dedicated resource for individuals with visual impairments, providing expert advice, support, and training on all things technology.
- [Visably](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/directory/visably/) This charity (previously known as British Wireless for the Blind Fund) provides audio devices, radios and audiobook players to visually impaired adults and children for free.
