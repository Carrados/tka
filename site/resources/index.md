## Knowledge Base

Recordings of our masterclass webinars and Career Talks, and in-depth articles on technology.

In the finished site, the recordings and articles are listed here, each with a transcript.

## Directory of services

Organisations and resources that help visually impaired people get the most out of technology, in fourteen kinds:

- Advice services
- Children and young people
- Coding and computing
- Events
- Grants
- Music
- Online resources
- Products
- Professional bodies
- Research
- Retailers
- Support at home
- Touch-typing software
- Trainers

In the finished site, each kind has its own page listing every organisation in it.
