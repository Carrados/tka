The Talking Kettle Association

Preview
This is a private preview of a website being built for a real charity, under an invented name. Names, pictures and numbers on it are made up.

We are a membership organisation which supports blind and partially sighted people to use technology at home, at work and in education.

We have over 700 members across the UK and internationally, from people just starting out with technology to leading experts in assistive and accessible technology. Professionals and family members are welcome too, as associate members.

It is free to join, so there has never been a better time to get involved, learn more about technology and have a say in the future of the organisation.

What can we help you with?

  • Get help with technology

    Email us or call our helpline, and we'll get back to you within 48 hours.

  • Free one-to-one training

    Up to five hours, for work, volunteering or study.

  • Join for free

    Full membership if you are blind or partially sighted, associate membership if you support us.

  • Come to an event

    Kettle Chat every month, masterclass webinars and Brew Break.

  • Find a service

    Our directory of organisations, trainers, suppliers and grants.
