It would be wonderful if you are able to support our work financially.

Your donation helps us provide free training in assistive technology to people as they move through education and towards work or volunteering.

If you are a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid adds 25p to every pound you give, at no cost to you.

In the finished site, a donation form here includes a Gift Aid box, so there's no need to email us separately. In this preview, nothing can be given.
