About us

Preview
This is a private preview of a website being built for a real charity, under an invented name. Names, pictures and numbers on it are made up.

The Talking Kettle Association (TKA) is a community of visually impaired people committed to improving access to mainstream and assistive technology.

We offer training, discussion, networking and help to anyone with a visual impairment.

Since 1969

We were founded in 1969 as The British Typewriter Club for the Blind (BTCB), the oldest computer association for visually impaired people in the world. We changed our name in November 2018, to welcome people who are partially sighted as well as people who are blind.

What we do

  • We promote the use of computers and technology by visually impaired people.
  • We encourage visually impaired people to explore what technology can do at home, at work and for leisure.
  • We share advances in technology, and find the resources and information that matter.
  • We encourage peer support among our members and among visually impaired people more widely.
  • We campaign for equal access to computers, technology and the internet.
  • We provide tutorials and training at every level of skill and interest.
  • We create networking opportunities for visually impaired people in many sectors, and hold events for members.

Who our members are

Everyone from people taking their first steps with technology to people working in the IT industry: people using technology for leisure, students at school or university, people using technology at work, developers, network architects and security specialists, researchers and academics, and organisations representing or building for visually impaired people. Our greatest asset is our members: with so much knowledge and experience among them, there is always someone who can help.

Working in partnership

We work with other organisations in the sight loss sector. We are a partner of Sightlines Network, which represents many local sight loss charities across the UK, and we are part of the technology working group of The Sight Loss Alliance, which brings together the national sight loss charities.

How we are run

We are a charitable company, incorporated in Scotland and regulated by the Scottish Charity Regulator, and we serve the whole of the UK. Our full members elect our trustees and vote at the Annual General Meeting. Our registered office is in Edinburgh, and our team works remotely.
