It is free to become a member, so there has never been a better time to get involved, learn more about technology and have a say in our future.

## Full and associate membership

- **Full membership** is for people who are blind or partially sighted. Full members elect the trustees who run the charity, and vote on proposals at the Annual General Meeting.
- **Associate membership** is for anyone else who supports our aims: professionals, family members and friends.

Together, members make sure everything we do is shaped by the experience of people with a visual impairment and of people working in the sector. They give the charity a powerful voice when we push for better products and services, and many share what they know through the discussion list, Kettle Chat and Brew Break.

## What every member gets

- Email updates on our webinars, events and technology news
- Contact with the charity at any time, by phone or email
- The email discussion list, where members ask questions and get informed, prompt answers
- Regular training events on Zoom, on topics members suggest
- One-to-one help at the monthly Kettle Chat
- Free one-to-one training, such as Technology for Work
- Discounts at Brew Break
