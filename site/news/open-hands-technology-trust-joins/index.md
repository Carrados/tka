5 November 2026

Open Hands Technology Trust (OHTT) became part of TKA on 30 October 2026.

"We have ambitious plans for the future," said Morgan Ashby, TKA Chair of Trustees. "The resources which are coming from OHTT will enable us to kick-start a number of new developments, and we are delighted to be able to build on OHTT's legacy."

OHTT's device scheme has now closed. If you would like advice on using technology, or on organisations that provide free equipment and grants for it, we can help: see [Equipment and funding](https://tka.unremarkable.me/supporting-you/equipment-and-funding/).
