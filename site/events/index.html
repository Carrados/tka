  • Masterclass: Agentic Accessibility

    Webinar, Wednesday 25 November 2026, 7.30pm

    For decades, accessibility has largely meant making technology easier for blind and partially sighted people to operate. But what happens when we can simply tell an AI agent what we want to achieve, and let it do the work? Robin Hale, blind for 40 years and an assistive technology trainer for 15 of them, reports back from months of hands-on experiments with today's AI agents: what works, what doesn't, and what might come next.
