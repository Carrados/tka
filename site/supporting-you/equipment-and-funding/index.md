If you need advice on using technology, or on organisations that provide free equipment and grants for it, we can help.

Email [hello@tka.unremarkable.me](mailto:hello@tka.unremarkable.me), or call 020 7946 0321 and leave a message.

## Two schemes that have joined us

- **Open Hands Technology Trust (OHTT)** became part of TKA on 30 October 2026. Its device scheme has now closed.
- **The Ringing Bell Fund** is now run by TKA.

For either, contact us in the same way, and we'll help you find what you need.

Our [directory](https://tka.unremarkable.me/resources/) also lists organisations that give grants and equipment.
